# Consent and retention gaps

Withdrawing a consent already refused a row that was not `granted`. The update now repeats `status = 'granted'` with the organization and healthcare organization. If that update matches nothing, the service returns `invalid_status` and does not write the withdrawal event or the audit. No representative, witness, or legal-hold field was added. D29-16 is still pending.

No retention period was chosen. D21-09 is still pending. Nothing deletes a document or a consent. A later approval has to name the record classes, the period, who may place a hold, and who may dispose after the hold ends.
