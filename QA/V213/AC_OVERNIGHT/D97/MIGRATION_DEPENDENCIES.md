# Migration dependencies

No migration was written and no database was changed. A migration stays unauthorized until the matching decision is approved and a separate migration approval is given. No migration filename is assigned here.

Areas that cannot be finished on the current tables alone, once a decision exists: patient merge lineage, retention and legal hold, a capacity constraint, and a clinical outbox. Offline writes, restore, break-glass, and Juflona also stay closed until their own approvals exist.
