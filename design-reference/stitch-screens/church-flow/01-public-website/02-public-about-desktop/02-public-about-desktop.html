Our Story

What began as a small gathering of faithful individuals has grown into a cornerstone of the Kafue community. Our journey has been one of unwavering trust and dedicated service, evolving to meet the needs of a changing world while remaining rooted in timeless values.

We believe that every person's story is valuable, and we strive to create a space where those stories can intertwine with the greater narrative of faith and hope.
