Kafue Baptist Church is more than just a building; we are a family dedicated to sharing the transformative power of the Gospel in Kafue and beyond.

The Church Story

Founded over three decades ago with a handful of faithful families, Kafue Baptist Church has stood as a beacon of hope along the Kafue River. Our journey has been one of unwavering faith, from humble open-air meetings to our vibrant, multi-generational campus today. We honor our heritage while looking forward to the future God has prepared for us.
