Our Story

Since our inception, Kafue Baptist Church has stood as a beacon of hope. What began as a small gathering of faithful believers has grown into a vibrant, multi-generational community dedicated to service and spiritual growth.

We believe in a "Structured Compassion"—an approach that combines the deep empathy of Christ's teaching with the excellence and organization required to serve a modern city effectively.
