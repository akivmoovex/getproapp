Our Story

Rooted in the vibrant community of Kafue, Kafue Baptist Church began as a small gathering of believers with a deep desire to share Christ's love. Over the years, we have grown into a diverse family dedicated to serving our neighbors and growing together in faith. Our journey is defined by God's faithfulness and the enduring spirit of our congregation.
