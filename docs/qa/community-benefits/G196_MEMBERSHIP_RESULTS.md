# G196 membership results

One membership is stored per person and program `activeclinic-community`. A second enrollment returns `duplicate: true`.

Enrollment starts at `pending_consent`. Activation requires recorded notice consent and a verification method other than `qr_scan`. Duplicate-person review also blocks activation. Zambia consent language is not approved, so the product does not activate a membership by itself.

Suspension and revocation block later eligibility and keep the history entries. Clinical charts are not merged across clinics.
