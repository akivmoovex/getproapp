# G233 clinical and pharmacy coverage

Existing ActiveClinic browser and HTTP coverage verifies worklist access,
encounter start, consultation draft/sign, vitals correction, prescription
creation, pharmacy queue behavior, stocked dispense, stock decrement,
wrong-patient/facility denial, and receptionist denial.

The full SOAP/referral field matrix is not independently covered for every
field. The stocked-dispense journey remains preserved and was rerun. No new
clinical approval, medication, inventory or dosage policy was invented.
This is an incomplete P1 depth gap rather than a production defect.
