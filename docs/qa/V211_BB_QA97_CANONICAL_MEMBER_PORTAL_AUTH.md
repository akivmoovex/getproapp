# V211-BB-QA97 Canonical Member Portal Authentication

## Result

`PARTIAL`. A safe compatibility bridge cannot be added without changing the
portal's underlying identity contract.

Canonical login uses the V5 platform session, regenerated by
`runTenantUnifiedLoginPost`, with a platform user ID. The current Member Portal
routes use `churchMember` from `src/church/memberAuth.js`; its session contains
the legacy numeric `member_id` and `branch_id`, and every portal handler passes
those values to legacy `membersRepo` queries.

The V2.11 member identity repository uses UUID member and branch identifiers
and is authorized by `requireActiveMemberForTenant`. It cannot safely be
serialized into the legacy session shape or consumed by the existing portal
queries. Creating such a bridge would either forge a legacy session or mix
incompatible identity schemas.

## Security decision

No product files were changed. The existing portal remains fail-closed:
canonical-only sessions do not satisfy `requireVerifiedMemberSession`; pending,
suspended, unbound, wrong-church, wrong-tenant, inactive-branch and blocked
members are not granted access through a fabricated context.

QA96's allowlisted login destination remains in place. Admin/staff role
destinations and tenant routing are unchanged.

## Required product boundary

The portal must either migrate its route/repository contract to the V2.11
UUID member identity and `requireActiveMemberForTenant`, or provide an
explicit, tested adapter that maps the canonical V2.11 identity to a legacy
portal member only when a server-side binding and branch/approval checks pass.
That adapter must not accept browser-supplied IDs and must preserve legacy
session validation.

No database or migration operation was executed. Static verification was
limited to inspection and `git diff --check`.
